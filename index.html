Our Values

At This Thing of Yours, your experience is just as important as the final product. We anchor our work in transparency and responsiveness, ensuring you are heard at every stage of the journey. We prioritize clear, consistent communication and a commitment to delivery timelines that respect the momentum of your celebration.

Above all, you are the singular focus of our day.

Why This Thing of Yours?

While many other companies operate on strict schedules and hourly constraints, we believe your story shouldn't be governed by a clock. A wedding day is a living, breathing event that shouldn't be burdened by the stress of filming windows or missed moments.

Our commitment is to the day, not the hour. No matter the package you choose, we are there from the first morning light until the final dance, ensuring every speech and every celebration is captured in full. To honor the entirety of your day, we include a complimentary string-out cut of every milestone moment.

For those hosting a post-wedding celebration the following morning, we offer an exclusive "Next-Day Premiere" service. For an additional fee, we will deliver an edited highlight reel overnight to share with your guests while the memories are still fresh.
